Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: We only store what’s needed for your account, your cards and the ranking. Your email address never shows up anywhere in the app – not on the card and not in the ranking. No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools.

2. What data we process

  • Account data: email address and password (stored only in encrypted form – we can’t read it either) or, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the identifier and email address Apple or Google sends us.
  • Profile data: the names of your connected Instagram, TikTok or Snapchat accounts, which of them appear on your card, your ranking settings (country, state, name for the ranking) and your acceptance of the terms.
  • Card data: your payments (amount and time), your Fame cards with serial number, gemstone and level.
  • Technical data: when you open the app or sign in, our service providers automatically process your IP address, date and time, and browser and device information (server log files).

3. Purposes and legal basis

  • Account, login, cards and ranking – so we can provide the app to you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, contract).
  • Security and operation (e.g. server log files, abuse prevention) – legitimate interest in a secure service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Connecting Instagram, TikTok or Snapchat and signing in with Apple or Google – only if you choose to (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).

4. What others can see

On your card and – if you join the ranking – in the ranking, the following is visible: the name of your chosen Instagram, TikTok or Snapchat account with the platform icon, your Fame amount or level, and your country and state. Anyone can check whether a card is real using its serial number. Your email address is never shown.

You only join the ranking if you confirm it in the ranking window. You can turn it off at any time via the ⚙ icon in the ranking.

5. Service providers

Supabase (account and database)

We store login, account and profile data with Supabase Inc. (USA). The database is hosted in a data center in Ireland (EU). A data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) is in place with Supabase; EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply to possible access from the USA. More: supabase.com/privacy.

GitHub Pages (app hosting)

The app is delivered via GitHub Pages by GitHub Inc. (USA). GitHub processes your IP address in server log files. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google Fonts

The app loads fonts from servers of Google Ireland Ltd. Your IP address is transmitted to Google in the process. More: policies.google.com/privacy.

Sign in with Apple or Google

If you choose “Sign in with Apple” or “Sign in with Google”, you are redirected to Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland) or Google (Google Ireland Ltd.). We then receive an identifier and your email address (with Apple, optionally an anonymous relay address). We never learn your Apple or Google password.

Instagram, TikTok und Snapchat

When you connect an account, you sign in with Meta (Instagram), TikTok or Snap (Snapchat). We only receive your public profile name, username, profile picture and an identifier – no messages, contacts, posts or password. While a connection isn’t set up yet, you enter your name yourself. When you share your card, the privacy policy of the respective platform applies to the shared content.

6. Storage on your device

The app stores your sign-in, a copy of your profile and cards, and settings like “sound off” and your language in your browser’s storage (Local Storage). This is necessary so the app starts quickly and also works offline (§ 25(2) TDDDG). We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Payments

There are no real payments yet. As soon as payments are possible, we’ll add here which payment provider processes which data.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your account, profile and card data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete this data unless we’re legally required to keep it (e.g. payment records). Our service providers delete server log files after a short time.

To delete your account, email us at the address above.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. If you live in the US, you may have additional rights under your state’s privacy laws (e.g. California).

10. Changes

If the app or the services we use change, we’ll update this privacy policy. The version published here applies.
